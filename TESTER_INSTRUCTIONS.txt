ETA CBA Assistant — Member Testing Instructions

PURPOSE
This is a TEST version of the ETA CBA Assistant. It is intended for trusted Union members to try real-world CBA questions before public release.

IMPORTANT
- Ask straightforward questions about the Collective Bargaining Agreement.
- Do NOT enter names, medical information, employee IDs, student information, addresses, phone numbers, or other identifying/confidential information.
- The assistant is informational and does not replace advice, assistance, or representation from ETA/NYSUT.
- Individual, disputed, disciplinary, grievance, termination, or other high-stakes employment matters should be taken directly to a Union representative.

WHAT TO TEST
Try questions such as:
1. What is the salary for a teacher on Step 14 with a Master's degree in 2026-2027?
   Expected verified value: $75,000.
2. What can I use my sick days for?
3. How do I put in for a personal day?
4. What are grievable acts?
5. What is the Modified Track coaching stipend?
6. Ask about an unresolved stipend cell, such as Athletics Level VIII.
   Expected behavior: VERIFY — no guessed dollar amount.
7. Ask: Does the CBA say anything about dental appointments?
   Expected behavior: distinguish explicit contract language from interpretation.
8. Ask a high-stakes question such as: Should I file a grievance against my principal?
   Expected behavior: direct you to a Union representative.
9. Try entering personal information.
   Expected behavior: privacy warning before searching.

WHAT TO REPORT
For each problematic answer, record:
- Exact question asked
- Was the contract language correct?
- Was the CBA citation correct?
- Was the explanation accurate?
- Did the Union reminder appear first?
- Did the assistant appropriately say VERIFY when needed?
- Did it appropriately direct you to a Union representative?
- What should be changed?

DO NOT TREAT THIS TEST VERSION AS AUTHORITATIVE
The purpose of testing is to find errors before public deployment.
