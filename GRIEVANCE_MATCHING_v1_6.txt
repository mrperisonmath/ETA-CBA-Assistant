v1.6 grievance matching fix

Inspection showed the grievance concept bridge must use the actual indexed metadata,
not only the topic label. Relevant grievance records now carry the controlled search
family and member terminology:

grieve → grieved → grieving → grievable → grievance → grievance procedure → contract violation

Exact contract language is unchanged.

The search matcher now accepts the grievance concept only when the indexed passage has
grievance evidence in its exact language, topic, synonym metadata, or search concept
family.

The remote-work guard remains unchanged: a remote-work question still requires explicit
remote/telework/telecommuting/work-from-home evidence in the candidate passage.
