Grievance concept family

The member-search vocabulary treats these as one controlled concept family:

grieve → grievable → grievance → grievance procedure → contract violation

Recognized forms include:
- grieve
- grieved
- grieving
- grievable
- grievance / grievances
- grievance procedure
- grievance process
- contract violation / contract violations

This is search metadata only. It does NOT alter or rewrite exact CBA language.

A grievance-family synonym may only bridge a query to a candidate passage when the
candidate already meets the conservative relevance threshold and the passage/topic
is substantively grievance-related. Generic word overlap remains insufficient.
