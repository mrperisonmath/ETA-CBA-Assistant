Doctor/Dental Appointment Regression Tests

Questions:
1. "Can I use a sick day for a doctor appointment?"
2. "Can I use a sick day for a dental appointment?"
3. "Do sick days cover dentist appointments?"

Required behavior:
- Retrieve the relevant sick-leave provision and show the CBA citation.
- Clearly state: "The contract does not specifically identify doctor or dental appointments."
- Do NOT say that the CBA definitely permits the appointment.
- Do NOT say that the CBA definitely prohibits it unless the contract expressly says so.
- Explain that the assistant should not infer coverage from general sick-leave language.
- Direct individual/unclear cases to a Union representative.
